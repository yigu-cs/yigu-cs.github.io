Welcome to my personal website!

I am a tenured associate professor in the Department of Computer Science at Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU). I received my M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from University of Memphis in 2008 and 2011, respectively. I had worked as an assistant professor of Computer Science at University of Tennessee Martin from 2011 to 2013 before I joined MTSU as an assistant professor in 2013. My main research interests include parallel and distributed computing, cloud/edge/green computing, cybersecurity, and wireless sensor networks.


  • Parallel and Distributed Computing
    • Workflow Scheduling
    • AI/ML in Multi-objective Optimization
  • Cloud/Edge/Green Computing
    • Energy Efficiency
  • Cybersecurity
  • Wireless Sensor Networks
