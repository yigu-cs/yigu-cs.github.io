Welcome to my personal website!

I am a tenured associate professor in the Department of Computer Science at Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU). I received my M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from University of Memphis in 2008 and 2011, respectively. I had worked as an assistant professor of Computer Science at University of Tennessee Martin from 2011 to 2013 before I joined MTSU as an assistant professor in 2013.

My research focuses on parallel and distributed computing, computer networking, cybersecurity and wireless sensor networks, with an emphasis on workflow scheduling, AI/ML-driven multi-objective optimization, cloud/edge/fog and green computing, AI for cybersecurity as well as IoT and network security.

  • Parallel and Distributed Computing
    • Workflow Scheduling
    • AI/ML in Multi-objective Optimization
  • Computer Networking
    • Cloud/Edge/Fog/Green Computing
  • Cybersecurity
    • AI in Cybersecurity
    • IoT and Network Security
  • Wireless Sensor Networks
